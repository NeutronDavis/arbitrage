3. GAS & COST MODEL SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All parameters are configurable via CLI and environment variables, flagged as assumptions:
- GAS_UNITS_ASSUMED (default: 500,000)
- CALLDATA_BYTES_ASSUMED (default: 1,000)
- FLASH_LOAN_FEE_BPS (default: on-chain reading 0.0 bps)
- SIZE_PROBE_MIN_BPS (default: -0.5 bps)

Formulas:
  L2 Gas USD = (GAS_UNITS_ASSUMED * l2_gas_price_wei / 1e18) * eth_usd_price
  L1 Data Fee USD = (l1_data_fee_wei / 1e18) * eth_usd_price
  Total Gas USD = L2 Gas USD + L1 Data Fee USD
  Flash Loan Fee USD = (FLASH_LOAN_FEE_BPS / 10,000) * best_size_weth * eth_usd_price
  Net Profit USD = best_profit_usd - Total Gas USD - Flash Loan Fee USD

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SIZE PROBE LADDER OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a block row's gross_spread_bps >= SIZE_PROBE_MIN_BPS (-0.5 bps):
1. A pinned Multicall3 batch is executed on that block height across 9 ladder sizes:
   [0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0] WETH.
2. Both legs are quoted on-chain to capture exact depth and price impact.
3. The size maximizing WETH profit is selected:
   profit_weth = size_weth * (spread_bps / 10,000)
4. Gas and flash loan fees are applied to compute net_profit_usd.
5. Record written to <OUTPUT_FILE>.probes.jsonl.
