     significantly tighter spreads.

     pool slippage on the shallower Pancake pools, widening the spread by 6–20 bps.

4. Gross Spread Reality (MEV & On-Chain Arbitrage):

   - This accurately reflects live Arbitrum market conditions: external arbitrageurs
     and CEX-DEX searchers continuously rebalance liquidity between venues.
